deckshoes 1.0.0 is the first stable release of the rewrite, and replaces 0.4's classes with functions over frozen data. The [migration guide](https://github.com/SimpleProgrammingAU/deckshoes/blob/main/MIGRATING.md) maps every 0.4 class, method and constant to its 1.0 equivalent, and names what 1.0.0 doesn't cover yet.

This section lists what's new since 1.0.0-alpha.3. The [changelog](https://github.com/SimpleProgrammingAU/deckshoes/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)'s 1.0.0-alpha sections list everything else in 1.0. From here, releases follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org).
